EnergySmart: electricity demand forecasting dashboard

A Streamlit app that forecasts hourly electricity demand, 6 to 168 hours ahead, with a Random Forest model trained on usage history and weather data (temperature, humidity and wind speed).

  • Model evaluated with MAE, RMSE and R². The dashboard compares actual and predicted use and shows usage trends, hourly patterns and Low, Medium and High usage breakdowns.
  • User registration and login with hashed passwords. Each user's readings are stored in SQLite, with CSV upload, manual entry and CSV export.
  • Includes a Tamil Nadu EB two-month bill estimator, explanations of why usage is high or low, and energy-saving tips. Available in English and Tamil, with light and dark themes.
  • Built with Codex as an AI coding assistant throughout development.

Built with Python, Streamlit, scikit-learn, pandas, NumPy, SQLite
